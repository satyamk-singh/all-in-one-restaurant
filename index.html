About us

All in One Restaurant is a family-run kitchen in Hussainabad, Palamu, owned by Satyam Singh. The idea is simple: one place where everyone at the table finds something they love, from a hot aloo paratha at breakfast to tandoori chicken at night and jalebi with rabri after.

Everything is cooked fresh to order. Pizzas are made with desi flavours, tikkas come off the clay oven, and the kids menu has its own little box.
